## Key evidence notes

*Ablating Safety* abstract and Sections 3–6 separate refusal, attempt, validated score, general score, spillover, instability; its Section 6 reports Qwen2.5-0.5B/1.5B/3B and SmolLM2-360M, with alpha `{0,.5,1,1.5,2}`. The refusal-direction abstract states erasing suppresses refusal and adding elicits refusal across 13 models, but does not establish calibration or continuous dose analysis. ACL 2026 uses a deployed-model censored/uncensored comparison, not controlled transformations. ACL metadata verifies XSTest’s 250 safe/200 unsafe prompts; OR-Bench arXiv metadata verifies 80,000 over-refusal, ~1,000 hard, and 600 toxic prompts.
