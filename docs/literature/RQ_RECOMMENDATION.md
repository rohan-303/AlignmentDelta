# RQ recommendation (charter unchanged)

| Existing RQ | Recommendation | Evidence/rationale | Recommended wording |
|---|---|---|---|
| RQ1 effects beyond refusal | Modify | Refusal effects are established; causal collateral attribution remains relevant. | Under a controlled same-checkpoint intervention, which dimensions change beyond refusal, and how reliably? |
| RQ2 mechanisms produce profiles | Modify | *Ablating Safety* already compares mechanisms. | Do intervention families produce distinguishable collateral-drift and restoration profiles under one protocol? |
| RQ3 strength affects outcomes | Retain/narrow | Strongest competitor already has strength/rank sweeps. | How do normalized strength and model family jointly shape refusal, competence, calibration, and robustness? |
| RQ4 willingness versus competence | Retain | StrongREJECT and *Ablating Safety* support the distinction. | When refusal decreases, does validated competence increase, or only attempt rate? |
| RQ5 inverse restoration | Retain/elevate conditionally | Refusal on/off is known; collateral restoration was not established in inspected sources. | Which collateral changes, if any, are restored by a technically valid inverse intervention? |

Merge RQ1–RQ3 into controlled drift/dose-response, retain RQ4, and make RQ5 the conditional differentiator. Formal charter changes are deferred.
