# Step 2.4 readiness levels

## ENGINEERING_GO

Permits Step 3 software/engineering validation on the pinned Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct revision. It does not authorize confirmatory benchmark collection, publication claims, or use of the full paper matrix.

Required gates: exact revision, local research terms/access, `trust_remote_code=False`, adapter, direction source/split, refusal score/tokens, site selection, intervention operator, random controls, and provenance/output schemas.

## CONFIRMATORY_GO

Permits paper-data collection only after every final model, license, benchmark, evaluator, item-manifest, consistency, statistics, exclusion, retry, and manual-validation gate is cleared.

The statuses are independent. Step 2.4 may produce `ENGINEERING_GO` with `CONFIRMATORY_BLOCKED`.
